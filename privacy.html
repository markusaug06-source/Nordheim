Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

What we collect

When you log in with Roblox, we receive your Roblox user ID, your display name and your Roblox headshot picture. When you book seats, we store the seat numbers, the concert, your display name and your Roblox user ID.

Why we collect it

We use this information to sign you in, show your name and picture on the site, link your seat bookings to your account, and stop one person from booking more seats than allowed.

Who can see it

Seat bookings show the seat and the display name of the person who booked it, so other visitors can see which seats are taken. Your user ID is only used by the site and is not shown publicly.

Where it is stored

Information is stored in Google Firebase, in Europe.

We do not

We do not sell your information, and we do not ask for your Roblox password. Roblox handles your sign-in; we only receive the information listed above.

Your choices

If you want your booking removed or have any questions about your information, contact us at the front desk or through the game's community chat.
